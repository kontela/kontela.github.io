SOURCES / METHODS

Public data. Visible boundaries.

  • GCAT launch log + object catalog: 2025 UTC launch dates, distinct launch IDs. 165 Falcon flights and 5 Starship tests. Mission groups are editorial and mutually exclusive. Flight payload averages sum catalog object masses and omit incomplete flights. Starlink counts require a real satellite name, not “Simulator.” Catalog updates can change historical counts.
  • SpaceX prospectus, June 5, 2026: reported launch classifications and payload throughput; 2025 Space and Connectivity results are shown here. 122 internal / 43 customer Falcon flights are company classifications, not inferred from every spacecraft’s owner.
  • Starlink availability JSON: provider labels, including territory codes and sub-national overrides. No inferred orbital coverage circles. Snapshot is not live.
  • Natural Earth: simplified geographic outlines, not an assertion about boundaries.
