17 relevant videos, drawn from 19 retrieved caption tracks. Founders, investors and creators discussing what they send, what they read, and what earns a reply.

The timestamps jump to the relevant passages inside longer interviews. Several uploads overlap; they are labeled rather than counted as independent evidence. Notes describe what speakers say, not verified success rates or universal rules. Full caption tracks were retrieved for local research; automatic captions can mishear names and numbers.
